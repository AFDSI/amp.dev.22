Fringe repairs

There is more to fringe than meets the eye:

  • Fringe is the skeleton of the rug
  • Each thread goes through the whole rug holding all the knots together
  • They can sometimes be annoying, but scissors would make very expensive consequences
  • Please call us if you wish to hide your rug's fringes or the fringe is damaged

We often get rugs with damaged fringes either because they got worn out through everyday usage or someone got tired of them and decided to cut them off. Any fringe related repairs require the rug to be placed into the loom and fringes have to be extended.
